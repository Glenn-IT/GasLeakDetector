Star Topology Power Distribution Setup

How Star Wiring & Diode Eliminate SIM800L Resets: The Buck Converter outputs 5.2V at up to 5A. Insert a 1N4007 diode on Branch 1 VCC (Anode to 5.2V, Cathode/silver stripe to SIM800L VCC) to drop voltage down to safe 4.5V. By running Branch 1 directly to the SIM module and Branch 2 directly to the Breadboard 5V Rail/Arduino, current bursts from the GSM module do not affect the Arduino's power supply.
