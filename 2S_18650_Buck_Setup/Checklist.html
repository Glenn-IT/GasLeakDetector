2S 18650 + Buck Converter Bring-Up Checklist

Gas Leak Detector · Safety & Step-by-Step Bring-Up Verification

Stage 0 — Buck Converter Calibration (CRITICAL)

Do NOT connect Arduino or SIM module before calibrating! A new Buck converter can output over 12V out-of-the-box if unadjusted, which will instantly destroy the Arduino and SIM module.
  • 2x 18650 Battery Pack (+ 7.4V) connected to Buck IN+ and (−) to IN-.
  • Multimeter probes placed on Buck OUT+ and OUT-.
  • Turned potentiometer screw until multimeter reads 5.0V ± 0.1V.

Stage 1 — Star Wiring Connections

  • Branch 1: Buck OUT+ and OUT- connected directly to SIM module 5V/VCC and GND using thick 18–22 AWG wire.
  • Branch 2: Buck OUT+ and OUT- connected to Breadboard +5V Rail and -GND Rail.
  • Arduino 5V and GND connected to Breadboard rails.
  • SIM TXD connected to Arduino D8.
  • SIM RXD connected to Arduino D7 (via 1kΩ / 2kΩ resistor divider).
  • Raw battery (+) through 100kΩ / 47kΩ divider connected to Arduino A1.

Stage 2 — Power On & Live Verification

  • Power on battery pack: Arduino power LED lights up cleanly.
  • LCD screen initializes and shows Gas: 83 B: 98% without flickering.
  • SIM module NET LED flashes slowly (registered on network).
  • Test SMS trigger: Message delivers cleanly to mobile phone with ZERO resets!
