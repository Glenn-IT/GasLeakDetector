Resistor Breadboard Wiring Guide

Visual step-by-step assembly diagrams for Battery Voltage Divider (A1), Alarm LED (D3), and GSM RX Level Divider (D7)

1. Battery Voltage Divider Wiring (100kΩ + 100kΩ)

Steps down 8.4V Raw Battery (+) to safe 4.2V Max for Arduino Pin A1 (Battery % sensing).
  1. 1Connect Resistor #1 (100kΩ): Connect Leg A to the Raw Battery (+) wire (8.4V). Plug Leg B into Row 15 on the breadboard.
  2. 2Connect Resistor #2 (100kΩ): Plug Leg A into the SAME Row 15. Plug Leg B into the GND (-) Rail.
  3. 3Connect Jumper Wire to A1: Plug one end of a jumper wire into Row 15 (where both resistors meet). Plug the other end into Arduino Pin A1.
Why this works: Row 15 acts as the middle junction point. It splits 8.4V in half to 4.2V, which is 100% safe for Arduino Pin A1.

2. Alarm LED Resistor Wiring (220Ω)

Limits current from Arduino Pin D3 to prevent burning out the LED or digital pin.
  1. 1Connect Resistor (220Ω): Connect Leg A to Arduino Pin D3. Plug Leg B into Row 22 on your breadboard.
  2. 2Connect LED: Plug the Longer Leg (+ Anode) of the LED into Row 22. Plug the Shorter Leg (- Cathode) into the GND (-) Rail.

3. GSM RX Signal Divider Wiring (1kΩ + 2kΩ)

Steps down Arduino D7 5V output to 3.3V for the GSM Module RXD pin.
  1. 1Connect 1kΩ Resistor: Connect Leg A to Arduino Pin D7. Plug Leg B into Row 28 on your breadboard.
  2. 2Connect 2kΩ Resistor: Plug Leg A into Row 28. Plug Leg B into the GND (-) Rail.
  3. 3Connect Jumper Wire to GSM RXD: Plug one end of a jumper wire into Row 28. Plug the other end into the GSM Module RXD Pin.
