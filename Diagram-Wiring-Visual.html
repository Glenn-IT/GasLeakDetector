🔋 2S 18650 Battery & Buck Powering

  • 2S Pack (7.4V nom, 8.4V max) supplies high continuous current.
  • LM2596 Buck Converter converts raw 7.4V/8.4V down to a stable 5.0V.
  • 1000µF Capacitor buffers the 2A GSM burst transmission spikes.
  • Common Ground: All negative lines (Arduino GND, Buck OUT-, Battery -, Sensor GND) connect together on the breadboard negative rail.

⚠️ Critical Prototyping Notes

  • Never power SIM900A from Arduino 5V: The Arduino linear regulator will overheat and reset during SMS transmissions.
  • A2 Battery Sense: Connect to raw battery (+) through the 100kΩ / 100kΩ divider, NOT the regulated 5V rail.
  • Dual MQ-6 Sensors: Area 1 on A0, Area 2 on A1, with common 5V and GND.
