Gas Leak Detector — Clean & Spaced Master Circuit Schematic

High-Resolution Visual Diagram with Ultra-Clean Wire Routing (Zero Crossing Clutter) · Arduino Uno · 2S 18650 · Buck Converter (5V) · Resistor Guides

⚠️ Critical Wiring Correction

Pin Assignment: A0 = MQ-6 #1 (Area 1) analog signal • A1 = MQ-6 #2 (Area 2) analog signal • A2 = Battery sense (voltage divider).
Original Error: Connecting Buck 5V directly to Arduino pin A2.
Why it fails: In GasLeakDetector.ino, pin A2 reads raw battery voltage to calculate Battery %. Connecting constant regulated 5V causes the display to permanently show 100% battery, hiding when your battery is dead.
Correct Wiring: Wire A2 to the RAW Battery (+) terminal BEFORE the Buck Converter through a 100kΩ / 100kΩ Resistor Voltage Divider.

2. Step-by-Step Breadboard Resistor Assembly Diagrams

Visual guide showing exact breadboard row connections for Blue Metal Film Resistors (5 Color Bands).

A. Battery Sensing Divider (100kΩ + 100kΩ) → Pin A2

  1. 1Resistor #1 (100kΩ): Connect Leg A to the Raw Battery (+) wire (8.4V). Plug Leg B into Row 15.
  2. 2Resistor #2 (100kΩ): Plug Leg A into the SAME Row 15. Plug Leg B into the GND (-) Rail.
  3. 3Jumper Wire to A2: Plug a jumper wire into Row 15 (where both resistors meet) and connect the other end to Arduino Pin A2.

B. Alarm LED Protection Resistor (220Ω) → Pin D3

C. GSM RX Signal Divider (1kΩ + 2kΩ) → Pin D7
