Direct 3.7V LiPo / 18650 Setup (No Capacitor Required)

Why this works without a capacitor: A high-discharge 3.7V 18650 or LiPo battery can easily supply 2A+ instant current bursts without voltage sag. To prevent SIM module restarts without adding a capacitor, you MUST use short, thick wires (18-22 AWG, < 10 cm) directly between the battery and the SIM module, bypassing the breadboard rails for power.
Common Ground Connection is mandatory: Connect the Battery (− / GND) directly to the SIM module GND AND tie a jumper wire from SIM GND to the Arduino GND / Breadboard − rail. Without a shared ground, TX/RX serial communication will fail.
